## INC300134 — I was just promoted to department head and need the same access as the previous manager

- **Priority:** High
- **Reported by:** Michael Brown (mbrown@servicedesk-simulator.com)
- **Department / Location:** Engineering / Floor 3
- **Contact:** x6270

### Issue Description
> HR just approved my promotion to department head, effective immediately. I need the same access the previous manager had — budget reports, approving purchase requests, and managing my team. Right now I only have standard department access, so I am blocked. HR said I should be added to the Management group.

### Troubleshooting Performed (per requester)
- HR confirmed the promotion via official email
- Requester currently has standard department access only
- Manager access was approved by the department VP

### Business Impact
Requester cannot approve pending purchase orders or access management tools; their team is waiting on approvals.

### Actions Taken
1. Opened the ticket from **My Queue** and clicked **Assign to Me**.
2. Opened **Tools → Directory** and searched for the requester ("mic").
3. Selected **Michael Brown** to open his user profile.
4. Went to the **Groups** tab on his profile and added him to the **Management** group.
5. Ticket automatically marked as resolved and removed from the queue.
6. Documented the actions taken

### Resolution
Added Michael Brown to the Management group via Directory → Groups, granting him the same access as the previous department head. Ticket closed.
